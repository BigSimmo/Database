CME mode — screen boards

Eleven screens for a continuing-education tracker, drawn phone-first. These settle the direction before any production file is touched — the same route On Call took.

Three of these are competing versions of the same home screen. Boards 1, 2 and 3 are alternatives, not a sequence. Pick one. Everything after board 3 is shared regardless of which home wins.

A shortfall is never red. The design system reserves the clinical status colours for clinical states, so being short of a target is shown by position, weight and plain words instead. That happens to be honest: three hours short is not an emergency.

What these boards are deliberately not showing

  • No streaks, no badges, no confetti. Every visual reward mechanic was left out on purpose. You take leave and you get sick; a broken chain would be an insult.
  • No red. Being short of a target is shown by position, weight and words. The clinical status colours stay reserved for clinical states.
  • No progress ring. Your design system ships a bar and nothing else, and a ring would mean building and registering a new component before a single CME feature existed. A bar says the same thing.
  • Nothing about what you typed. Every capture surface shows document titles only. The one screen where that could be misunderstood — Drafts — states it in plain words on the screen itself.
  • No hour figures the app invented. Every number on board 11 is one you confirmed, dated and attributed. The numbers shown here are drawn from search summaries and have not been verified against the College's own document.
