On Call · phone screens · v6

On Call, a dashboard for the shift

Eleven screens at 390 px in the app's own tokens. Home is now seven modules — shift progress, the calls that come first, tonight's wards, a pinned reminder, recent numbers, what's coming, and the section grid. Both rows of chrome stay the app's own, read out of master-search-header.tsx, mode-nav.tsx and nav-slot-ink.tsx rather than redrawn. No search, no bottom toolbar.

All content is example data. Numbers, wards and sessions are placeholders for layout only — no real internal contact, and no credential in any field.
How the chrome is stacked
1 · Universal header
The app’s own row, unaltered: 56 px tall, hamburger on the left, the mode pill centred — 48 px, fully round, a 32 px filled blue circle around the mode glyph — and a header control on the right. The "Mode" eyebrow is hidden at phone width, exactly as the component does it.
2 · The switcher, altered
In On Call the switcher opens this mode's seven sections first, instead of the flat list of modes. That is the one thing this mode changes about the universal header.
3 · The section rail
Tonight, Contacts, Playbook and More, on a 48 px bar. Every slot keeps one weight in every state — bolding the current label would change its width and shift the rule beneath it. The rule hangs off the word, not the slot.
4 · New on Home
Shift progress, a scrolling strip of tonight's wards, and a recent-numbers list — the three additions that save the most taps on a second night. The freshness warning has moved to Contacts, where the fix is.

Quick

All sections

  • Seven modules, each with a quiet monospaced label and its own action where one helps. The shift module is the only one painted in the mode's purple — it is context, not content, and the tint says so without a border shouting it.
  • Shift progress is new and earns its place on nights: a thin bar, the start time, and how long is left. It answers "how much of this is still ahead of me" without arithmetic at 3am.
  • Your wards is new — a scrolling strip of just tonight's wards with their extensions, so the three numbers you dial most are one tap from the top of the app.
  • Recent is new, and is probably the highest-yield module here: on a second night you re-dial the same four numbers, and this removes the search entirely.
  • The consultant sentence is pinned in accent rather than buried in the Playbook. It is the hub's single most valuable line, so it gets a card.
  • The freshness warning is gone from Home. It belongs on Contacts, where the overdue rows actually are and where the fix is one tap away.
