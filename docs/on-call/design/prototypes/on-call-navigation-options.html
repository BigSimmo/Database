On Call — what the second row should actually be

The rail under the header lists Tonight, Contacts, Playbook and More. The mode pill above it opens the same nine destinations. Two controls, one job, stacked on top of each other — that is the fault, not the rail's styling.

The division of labour these boards assume. The pill moves you between On Call's pages, which it already does. The second row moves you around the page you are on — the groups inside it — which nothing does today. Every board below is Contacts at 390 px; only that second row changes.

What I would do

The correction that matters
Whatever shape wins, the nine sections stop being listed twice. They live in the pill, which already opens them. That alone removes the second grey band and the "More" overflow.
Recommendation
A. It is a component the app already ships and three other modes already use, so it is adoption rather than new design. It answers a question nothing currently answers — where am I in this page — and it costs 17 px on a title row the page needs regardless.
If you want it always visible
B, but I would then move the section's filter chips into the page menu. Two pill rows one above the other, one jumping and one filtering, is a mis-tap waiting to happen on the page that matters most.
If you would rather have the space
C. Perfectly defensible on the short pages — Teaching, Orientation, Who's who — and it is genuinely the most content on screen. It is only Contacts and Logistics that are long enough to want a jump. A and C could even coexist: the header appears only where the page has more than three groups.
