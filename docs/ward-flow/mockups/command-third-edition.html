    Override register

    A judgement about the patient is overridable by a named coordinator with a recorded reason. A fact about the world is not: no reason typed into a form creates a bed, frees one-to-one nursing, or undoes a ward’s decline. The split is fail-closed, so a check nobody has classified as a judgement is not overridable. Each override below is recorded with its reason and reviewed at governance.
