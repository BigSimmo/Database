  • One ward's own figures, reached from that ward's home screen. Nothing on it aggregates across wards and nothing on it writes anything back.
  • The ward's identity, Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital, adult acute, beds, all open, no locked bay, is the repository's own, so this page and the ward home screen cannot contradict each other.

    How the figures agree with each other

    The page counts its own figures on every load and prints the result in the rail. Each line below is one of those checks, stated as the sum it makes.

      What this page does not knowstated, not guessed

      Where a discharge went, home or another inpatient service or a community bed, is not recorded by this ward, so no split is shown for it. That is different from a nought. A nought means the count was taken and came back none. This means the count was never possible to take.

      Nothing on this page is extrapolated, projected or compared against another ward. Every scale is stated, every window is stated, and no figure is carried outside the window it was counted in.
