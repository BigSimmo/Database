    Built to the design system, second edition

    AQuiet bar

    • One row. The title, the mark and the disclaimer, then search, then the scope, then the corner.
    • The core figures are the control. Press them and every figure for this page drops down, in groups, with the reconciliation line.
    • Tasks and Activity are two quiet controls with a count and a dot. Each drops a panel under itself.
    • The lightest of the three. It reads like a toolbar and leaves the most room for the page.

    BInstrument corner

    • The scope, the core figures, the freshness line and All figures sit in one bordered cluster, so the state of the page reads as one instrument.
    • Tasks and Activity open as drawers from the right edge with the page dimmed behind them, so a long task list has a full column to itself.
    • The primary action sits before the cluster, so the eye lands on the figures last, at the corner.
    • The most instrument-like of the three, and the one that keeps the most on screen at once.

    CTab strip

    • A strip beside the title holds the page's own view, then Tasks, Figures and Activity as tabs. The current view carries the gilt underline.
    • A tab opens a shelf that spans the whole header and pushes nothing, so the queue stays where it was.
    • Search sits to the right and grows when it is used. The corner shows only the breached and the waiting counts.
    • The most navigational of the three, and the one that gives the popups the most width.

    Shared by all three

    • Universal search finds patients by name or identifier, and movements, departments, wards and owners. Press the slash key to reach it. It refuses a risk or acuity score, a best match, and closed or arrived movements, and says so.
    • The figures are scoped to the page. Command shows waiting, breached and due soon. Capacity shows beds free, sites with none and beds held. Referrals shows the triage list. Use the rail to switch.
    • Tasks carries every piece of open work, worst first, each a filter on the queue, with the notices above it. A notice is marked seen with one press.
    • Activity says what is going on in a sentence derived from the data, then the last events with their times, under a freshness line.
    • More holds the handover sheet, the ward contacts pop-out, the design system, the appearance and sign out. New referral is the one primary action.
    • Escape closes a popup first, then the search results, then clears the search, the selection, the task and the scope, in that order.

    Every figure here is invented: the 23 movements, their identifiers, tiers, waits, deadlines, owners and destinations, the four referrals, the bed counts, the four overrides, the notices, the activity, the clock and the shift.

    Every person's name is invented, built from uncommon given names and words for plants, birds and stones so that none matches a real person. The ward contacts pop-out shows placeholder extensions and addresses in the shape the live product would use. No real phone number, address or record number appears anywhere on this screen.

    What is real: the eight emergency departments, the hospital sites, the health services and the ward names, from the repository's own tables. Six of the seventeen sites are drawn here.
