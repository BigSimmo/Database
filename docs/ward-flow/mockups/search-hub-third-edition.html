Servicescopes the directory

One service, or all of them. The directory, the tabs, the drawers, the rail and the reconciliation line follow the choice. The standard names four health services and gives each one a colour. St John of God Subiaco is a private hospital and is not one of them, so it is listed here and drawn without a service colour rather than given an invented fifth hue.
