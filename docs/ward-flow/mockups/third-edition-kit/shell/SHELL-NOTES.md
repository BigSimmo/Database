# Shell notes: what changed, how to graft it, what is left

The owner's universal header and rail (`inputs/rail.html`) restyled to the third edition as graft
ready fragments. The review that drove the changes is `inputs/SHELL-REVIEW.md`. Everything here
was verified on `shell/preview.html` with `shell/check-preview.mjs` (the third edition harness
adapted for a page that is not Command) and looked at in both themes at 1920 by 1080, 1440 by 900,
1280 by 800 and 1100 by 800 under `docs/ward-flow/mockups/third-edition-shots/shell-preview/`.

## What changed and why, by the brief's rules

**Identity tokens and faces (brief, tokens and font link).** The shell names no hex and no face.
Every colour is a token and every face is `--display`, `--body` or `--mono`, so it takes Source
Serif 4, Source Sans 3 and JetBrains Mono from the third edition block. One `#000` remains, inside
the `mask-image` gradient on `.railScroll`, where it is an alpha ramp and not a colour on screen,
copied from the mockup's own fade rule for its lists.

**Loaded weights only.** `.hdr1 h1` serif 500 became 600. The wordmark and "WF" take the base
block's `.brand b` at `--t-5` 600. No other serif weight is asked for. Sans 500 and mono 500 stay
where the artifact had them, because both are loaded.

**Rule 1 and 2, canvas and stripe.** Untouched. The stripe's `z-index` is raised to 45 in
`shell.css` so the bar (31), the hover cards (35) and the drawers (40) never paint over it, per the
owner's decision on C.9. Pointer events stay off. The skip link goes to 50.

**Rule 3, one elevation step.** The menu panel, the search results, the hover card and the drawers
each take `var(--lift)` plus the inset highlight and the hairlines, once. The chosen segment inside
the Activity drawer lost its second `var(--lift)` and takes a hairline ring and the brass underline.
The shift block and the tally tiles are strips on `--surface-2` with a hairline and no shadow.

**Rule 4, strips.** Drawer heads (`.popHead`), drawer feet (`.popFoot`), the tally foot
(`.statsFoot`) and the table heads sit on `--surface-2`. The menu panel clips its corners with the
panel radius, so its strips need no inner radius of their own.

**Rule 5, hairlines.** Every line reads `--line` or `--line-strong`. The artifact's solid greys came
from its base block, which the graft must not carry (RAIL-MAP d.5).

**Rule 6, brass is a bar.** Brass appears only as the bar on the chosen service row, the pressed
task row, the current rail item, the chosen block in the drawn layout and the current card, as the
underline under the chosen segment, and as the two letters beside the wordmark. No `--gilt-soft`
anywhere.

**Rule 7 and 9, selection is a ring, a count of none is a word.** The open summary, the chosen
service row, the pressed task row, the pressed pinned row, the current rail item, the open pinned
fly out and the first search result all carry `inset 0 0 0 1px var(--accent)`. Tags and pills
carry `data-zero="true"` when the count is none and are drawn as the italic word with no fill and
no ring. A new notice keeps the fill without the ring, because it is not a selection (C.11).

**Rule 8, buttons.** Every summary, the Close control and the rail's flip control are secondary
buttons: `--surface`, 1 px `--line-strong`, radius `--r2`, hover and press to `--sunk` with an
ink-soft border. The primary control (New referral) fills with `--accent`, carries
`inset 0 1px 0 var(--hl-on-accent)`, and goes to `--accent-ink` on hover, press and open. Nothing
brightens on hover in either theme.

**Rule 10, scrollbars.** Thin, `--line-strong` thumb, transparent track, on `.railScroll`, the
drawer panels and `.tableWrap`.

**Rule 11, titles.** Drawer heads are the display serif at `--t-3` 600 in `--accent-ink`, like a
panel title. The design system's card titles and the prose column headings in `shell-docs.css`
are the same.

**Type rules.** The strip's word, its tags, the pinned fly out's tag and the ring's percentage all
moved from 9 px or 9.5 px to `--t-0`. The closed brand moved from 19 px to `--t-5`. Uppercase labels
carry 0.08 to 0.12 em. Measured with Source Sans 3 at 10.5 px the eleven strip words fit in 76 px
with room to spare (Governance is the widest), so `--railw-closed` stays at 76 px. If a future
word does not fit, widen `--railw-closed` in `shell.css`. Words never shrink.

**Focus and names (rule 10 of the mockup's own list).** Every control takes the base block's
`:focus-visible` ring, and the search field draws it on `.search:focus-within`. The Service
summary lost its `aria-label` and gained a hidden "Service: " prefix so its value is read. The
Activity and Tasks dots are `aria-hidden` with a hidden sentence beside each ("live" or "quiet",
"2 new notices, one a breach" or "no new notice"). The strip's flip control and check sentence are
clipped rather than `display: none`, so their words stay in the accessible name. The open rail's
tone dot gets a hidden "a legal deadline has passed" or "look here" after the state line.

**Tap targets.** `.qHit`, `.taskRow`, `.toolItem`, `.pinRow`, `.seg button`, `.closeBtn`,
`.railBtn`, `.clearBtn` and `.seen` joined the 3 rem rule at a coarse pointer.

**Drawer backdrop (C.13).** The 0.6 opacity is gone. The backdrop is `var(--edge-shade)` at full
strength: measured `rgba(30, 48, 66, 0.16)` in light and `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)` in dark. In light
the drawer still reads as over the page because of its lift and its heavier left hairline. If the
owner wants a deeper light shade, stack the token twice in `background-image` (about 0.29) rather
than adding a colour.

**The rail state (RAIL-MAP d.6).** One `nav.rail`. `data-rail="open|closed"` lives on the root and
is stamped before first paint by a head snippet (see the graft), so there is no width jump. The
rail's own `railBtn` and the bracket key call one `setRail()`, which re-renders, moves focus to the
new `railBtn`, announces "The rail, closed." or "The rail, open.", and stores `ward-flow-rail`. No
switcher, no number keys, no `railCap`, no `both`. At 1000 px and narrower the rail renders in the
open shape as a wrapping row whatever is stored, and the toggle is hidden. The rail's scrolling
middle fades at the edge it continues past, measured like the mockup's lists, so the Pinned group
is never hidden at 900 px tall without a sign.

**The date (C.15, owner decision).** The shift block's eyebrow reads "Day shift" with 10:42 at its
right, and the line under the time to handover reads "to handover at 14:00, Sat 15 Aug". The Tools
drawer's signed in line carries the same date with AWST.

**Service scope (RAIL-MAP d.4 item 9, owner decision).** The selector scopes the queue (through
`visible`), the pressure strip (through `edVisible`), the drawers, the rail, the contact tables and
the reconciliation line. The diagram is not scoped: `diagramNote()` returns "Showing the whole
network. The queue is scoped to South Metropolitan." for the graft to place in `.diagFoot`. The
selector's head reads "scopes the queue and the strip" and its note says the Statewide flow shows
the whole network.

**One source of truth (RAIL-MAP d.8, owner decision).** `MOVES`, `REFERRALS`, `BEDS`, `WARDS`,
`EXTRA`, `OVERRIDES` and `window.__headerCheck` are gone. Beds by site are summed from `UNITS`
(free is `ready`, held is `held`). Ward contacts are `UNITS`, department contacts are `EDS`. Events
are derived from waits, deadlines, declines (`declines[].at`), escalations, referrals raised and
triaged, and the override register. Notices are the override register and breaches within the last
hour. The shell's three sum checks and a check that every pinned id exists are appended to the
engine's `window.__commandCheck` for the rail foot and the tally foot, and the shell never creates
that array.

**Escape (S.3.18, S.4.24).** The shell owns one order: an open pop out, the search results, the
search text, the engine's ward selection, the engine's referral subject, the engine's department
filter, the task filter, the service. A search field clears itself on Escape and would fold two
steps into one, so the field's own keydown takes the key and walks the order.

**Copy.** No em dash, semicolon or arrow in anything the shell says. "Owners" left the search
placeholder because the engine records no owner. The Design system link points at the sibling
`design-system-third-edition.html` and says "third edition". The pinned fly out's note now says
pinning from a row is not wired (S.16.4).

## The graft, step by step

Path A of RAIL-MAP part e, into `docs/ward-flow/mockups/command-third-edition.html`.

1. **Head.** In the head script that restores the appearance, add one line so the rail state is
   stamped before first paint:
   `if (localStorage.getItem("ward-flow-rail") === "closed") document.documentElement.setAttribute("data-rail", "closed");`
   inside the existing `try`.
2. **Stylesheet.** Append the whole of `shell.css` after the mockup's own rules, inside the same
   `<style>` (or as a second `<style>`). Do not carry the artifact's base block. `shell-docs.css`
   goes to the design system page, not to Command.
3. **Markup.** Replace `div.app` with `shell-markup.html` and put the mockup's body where the
   comment says: the Emergency department pressure section, `div.grid3` (queue panel with
   `#qpane-patients`, `#qpane-referrals`, `#qFilter`), `div.midCol` and `section#slPanel`, all
   unchanged. This replaces the old `header.topbar` (its `h1`, `.chip.mark`, `.sub`, `dl#kpis` and
   `.clock`) with `header.hdr1`, and the old static `nav.rail` (its three groups, `#railOpen`,
   `#railGov`, the `div.railFoot` with `#railCheck`, the `.apRow` appearance group `#appearance`
   and the `.railNote`) with the empty `nav#rail`. The skip link keeps `#qpane-patients`. The
   `#live` paragraph is the shell's.
4. **Engine changes**, all inside the engine's IIFE:
   - `renderFigures()`: drop the writes to `#railOpen` and `#railGov` (the elements are gone).
   - `queueRows()`: after the `edFilter` step add
     `if (window.WardFlowShell) rows = rows.filter(window.WardFlowShell.visible);`
   - `renderEds()`: filter `rows` with `window.WardFlowShell.edVisible` when the shell exists.
     `reconcile()` calls `edPressure()` itself, so its waiting sum stays whole network.
   - `renderQueueTabs()`: after it writes `#qFilter`, append
     `window.WardFlowShell.filterBar()` to `f.innerHTML` when the shell exists.
   - `renderDiagram()` or the diagram foot: write `window.WardFlowShell.diagramNote()` into
     `#diagScrollNote`'s sibling or a new `span.diagScrollNote` in `.diagFoot` while it is not
     empty.
   - `renderAll()`: as its last line, `if (window.WardFlowShell) window.WardFlowShell.render();`
   - The Escape listener: first line `if (window.WardFlowShell) return;`
   - The click listener's `.railLink` branch: `if (window.WardFlowShell) return;` before it, or
     delete the branch, because the shell announces those clicks.
   - The block that writes `#railCheck` after `window.__commandCheck = reconcile();` can stay
     (the shell renders its own `#railCheck` from the same array on every render) or go.
   - Delete the mockup's appearance script at the end of the file. The shell's own script carries
     it, on the same key `ward-flow-command-appearance`, document level, so the group inside Tools
     works after every re-render.
5. **The facade.** At the end of the engine's IIFE, after `renderAll()`, expose:
   ```
   window.WardFlow = { NOW, MOVEMENTS, UNITS, EDS, REFERRALS, OVERRIDES, state, isOpen, isBreached,
     ed, unit, byId, dur, referralQueueOrder, announce, renderAll, selectMovement, selectQueueTab };
   ```
   `EDS` and `UNITS` already carry `service` ("East Metro", "North Metro", "South Metro", "WACHS")
   and `REFERRALS` carry `homeService`. Nothing needs adding to the data for the scope to work.
6. **The script.** Add `shell-script.js` as a script after the engine's script. It calls
   `WardFlow.renderAll()` once on load so the queue is rendered with the predicate installed.
7. **The harness.** `check.mjs` clicks `.apBtn >> text=Light` without opening Tools, so on the
   grafted page it times out. Open `#toolsMenu summary` before the click (one line, as in
   `check-preview.mjs`), or move the appearance control somewhere always visible, which would be a
   change to the build sheet's section 9.6.
8. **Verify** with the list in RAIL-MAP part e: `reconcile` green with one `window.__commandCheck`,
   the typefloor, the diagram at 1440 by 900 (the header is 56 px, the mockup's flex column absorbs
   it), the rail's tags equal to the queue and register counts, search selecting a row in
   `#qpane-patients`, and the rail state surviving a reload.

## The predicate and the queue

`WardFlowShell.visible(m)` is true when the movement is in the chosen service (its department's
service), passes the pressed task's test if one is pressed, and matches the search text if there
is one and the text is not a refusal. The engine applies it in `queueRows()` after its own ED
filter, so the header count, the tab count, the filter bar and the rows all agree. Choosing a
person from the results clears the search, clears an ED filter that would hide the row, shows the
patients pane, calls `selectMovement(id)`, scrolls the row into view and focuses it.

## What the preview stubbed

`preview-stub.js` stands in for the engine with eight movements (one arrived and so closed, one
breached, three due within two hours, one accepted awaiting a bed, two carrying invented names),
six wards across five sites (two with no ready bed), the eight departments, four referrals (one
triaged) and two overrides. Its `renderAll` draws a plain patient queue with `.qRow[data-mv]`
rows honouring the predicate, writes the shell's filter bar into `#qFilter`, writes the diagram
note into the preview's first panel, and calls the shell back. `selectMovement` sets the subject
and re-renders. It defines no `window.__commandCheck`, so the harness treats the preview as not a
Command page, which it is not. The preview's `.scroll` holds two panels so the mockup's locked
laptop layout has its two grid rows.

## Unresolved

1. **Wait format.** The rail and drawers use the engine's `dur()` ("4 h 28 m") so one screen writes
   a wait one way. The build sheet's S.14.9 wants "25h 10m". One of the two should change, and it
   is the owner's call which.
2. **Names.** The engine's movements carry no invented names, so search by name works only once
   the graft adds `given` and `family` to `MOVEMENTS`. Until then the results show the identifier
   as the name, and the pinned rows show the site and tier.
3. **Owners.** The engine has no owner field. The "with no owner" task, the owners group in
   search and the "Movements with no owner" fact appear only when a movement carries
   `owner: null` explicitly. Nothing is invented meanwhile.
4. **Pinning from a row** is not wired (S.16.4), and the engine always keeps one selected
   movement, so a pinned row cannot clear the selection by a second press. It re-selects.
5. **Notices.** Two of the three kinds in S.16.6 can be derived (an override recorded, a deadline
   passed within the hour). A bed pulled has no time in the engine's data.
6. **The appearance control in Tools** fails the unadapted `check.mjs` (see step 7 above). Either
   the harness opens Tools first or the control moves.
7. **Other screens.** Pressing another screen in the rail announces that it is not part of the
   prototype and switches the live tally to that screen's figures. The title stays "Command"
   because the body does. The artifact's drawn layout for that screen is design system content.
