    7 · Rules the build must not break

    Each of these is here because breaking it has already caused a defect somewhere on this system.

    1. 1Tap targets stay at 48px. Not 44. Reducing them to satisfy a generic accessibility rule reintroduces a known test flake.
    2. 2The reason sits beside the Send button, never above it. Reasons appear and disappear as you answer, and a reason above the button moves the button out from under a thumb.
    3. 3A figure beside a checkbox stays beside that checkbox, and a destination’s question stays on that destination’s card. The screen reader announcement points at it, and moving it into a rail silently unhooks the description.
    4. 4Absence is written in words. “Not recorded”, “Not written yet”, never a blank, never a dash, never a zero. A blank reads as a value.
    5. 5Nothing on this screen ranks, scores or suggests a destination, and nothing scores the history. Ordering is the network’s own fixed order, and the copy says so.
    6. 6The name never enters the structured referral, and the history is not structured. Say which half is enforced, every time it is said.
    7. 7Every list of options is generated from the one source of truth, never retyped into a sentence. Retyped enumerations produced stale copy three times, including the sentence on this very screen that counted the permitted facts and was still saying “five” after a sixth was added.
    8. 8The rail reports the history. It never previews it. A truncated preview implies that something read it.
    9. 9Free text is never given a green state. Green means checked on this screen, and nothing checks this.
    10. 10Nothing truncates the history silently. Over the limit is a blocking, visible, counted state.
    11. 11When more than one destination is chosen, the screen says the history goes to all of them, before the send and not after.
    12. 12Removing a statement from the screen relocates it. It does not delete it. v6 took the banner off the top of the page at the owner’s request. The prototype mark, the free text warning and the not a medical device sentence all still exist, in three places a reader actually reaches. Check this rule before removing any other sentence for reasons of appearance.

    8 · Open questions the build must settle

    Written down here rather than promised on screen. A screen that guarantees behaviour nobody has built is the defect this project finds most often.

    What happens to a half written history if the page reloads? Today it is lost, because a reload wipes everything. Tolerable for three checkboxes, not tolerable for four paragraphs of a risk note. Either the draft is held and the screen says so, or the screen warns before leaving. It must not silently do neither.
    Can the history be edited after sending, and does the receiving team see that it changed? Unanswered. If it can be edited, the version the receiving team read must remain recoverable, or two people will be acting on different accounts of the same patient.
    Who can read the history after it is sent, and for how long? The structured fields carry no identifying content, so access has been a low stakes question. The history changes that: it is the first thing on this form that would matter if the wrong person read it. Needs an answer before a real patient, not before it is built.
    Are the three sections the right three, and are the limits right? Chosen as the smallest set that shapes the writing without becoming a clinical form: why now, background, risk and safety. 1500, 2000 and 1000 characters are placeholders. Nobody has measured a real referral against them. Both are clinical judgements and belong to the owner.

    What is invented

    Maya Whitlock, her record number, her date of birth, her suburb, her catchment team and every word of the written history. The 41 minute median answer. The character limits of 1500, 2000 and 1000. The twelve questions and the answers standing against them. Every movement, referral, ward bed count, clock and figure the rail, the bar and the drawers show, which are the Command screen’s own invented data. This form is not a medical device, and it does not assess, score, rank or diagnose.

    What is real

    The hospital sites, the emergency departments, the ward names and the four health services are real WA names from the repository’s own tables. The screen is drawn mid flight on purpose, so answered, outstanding, not applicable, in catchment, out of catchment, refused, written and unwritten states are all visible at once. The patient box’s fields are the ones a person record genuinely holds, and where a record does not hold one it says so in words rather than leaving a gap.

    What changed from the locked v6 design

    The page’s own top strip is replaced by the universal bar and rail, which carry the back path to Referrals, the page title and the prototype mark. Every count on this page is now derived from the page’s own data rather than typed, which moved the progress figures from 10 of 12 answered with 2 outstanding to the figures below, because the locked page’s Send button already named three unanswered questions. Every em dash, semicolon and arrow is gone, the destination figures are read from the Command network rather than invented separately, and the yes and no controls, the destination choices and the department’s question are buttons a keyboard can reach. Nothing that was asserted is now unasserted.
