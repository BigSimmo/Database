About this viewwhat this page is, and is not

This is FSH Adult Secure’s own view. Every figure and every referral below belongs to this ward, never another one. No bed is ever allocated automatically: a person here confirms every step, an unanswered question never closes the ward, and most rules that refuse a referral record the refusal rather than blocking the decision.

The ward states, movements, referrals, clocks and figures are invented. The hospital sites, the health services and the ward names are real WA names. The page says so again in its foot, where what is real and what is invented are listed apart.
