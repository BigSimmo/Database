Every figure here is invented

  • Every wait, count, distribution and median on this screen is derived from the same invented movements Command reads: the thirteen people standing in a department at 10:42 on Saturday 15 August, their waits, their tiers, their legal deadlines and the ward each has or has not been accepted into.
  • The last seven days and the last thirty are invented and were kept from the source page, because Command carries no history at all: the forty one acceptances and the seven placements out of area, how those split between the eight departments, the two ward breakdowns, and the fifteen sampled medians behind the thirty day chart. Only the chart's last point is derived, and it is today's median.
  • The people are invented, family name first, from the same plant, bird and stone names Command uses. No identifier, name, phone number or address on this screen belongs to anybody.

What is real

  • The eight emergency departments and their codes, which are Royal Perth, Sir Charles Gairdner, Fiona Stanley, Armadale, St John of God Midland, Rockingham General, Joondalup Health Campus and Peel Health Campus. They are real Western Australian sites, and they are the same eight, with the same health service against each, that Command reads from ward-sites.ts.
  • The wards named in the ward breakdowns are the sixteen wards Command carries, so this screen cannot send somebody to a ward that does not exist. Bunbury Regional Hospital is a real WA Country Health Service site and is named as an out of area destination only, because no country site is drawn in this network.
  • The four health services, which are East Metropolitan, North Metropolitan, South Metropolitan and WA Country. All four are real, and the Service selector in the bar scopes this whole screen to one of them.
  • The visual language is the Ward Flow third edition standard, copied from command-third-edition.html: the same stylesheet, the same bar, the same rail in both of its states, the same tokens and the same seven step scale.

What was reconciled against Command, and what changed

  • The day. The source page stood at 11:42 on an unnamed day with sixty eight people waiting, three of them past twenty four hours and the longest at Royal Perth on thirty one hours forty. This page stands where Command stands, at 10:42 on Saturday 15 August, day shift, handover at 14:00, and every current figure is Command's.
  • Waiting now. Thirteen people are standing in a department. Command's rail counts fourteen open movements, and the fourteenth, WF-031, has left St John of God Midland and is moving to FSH Adult Secure, so it is named under the waiting list rather than counted in it. Command's own pressure strip counts the same thirteen.
  • Over twenty four hours reads none, and that is a measurement. Nobody in this network has been waiting more than eight hours today, so three of the five wait bands are empty. They are still drawn, and they still read none, because a band that vanishes when it is empty is a band a reader cannot trust when it is not.
  • A median needs three people. Every department has one or two waiting today, so no department shows a median and each says so in words. The network median over all thirteen is the one figure worth comparing against, and it is in the band above and at the foot of the comparison table.
  • The department switcher is now a tab strip with a derived count against every department, and it is the same control the Escape key clears, in the shell's own order.

A department is not a ward

This screen only ever describes people standing in a department: how many, how long, and where they went next. It never shows a bed count, an occupancy figure or a length of stay for a department, because a department has none of its own. Those belong to Capacity and to the ward screens.

What a nought means, and what a stated absence means

A nought, written none, is a measured answer: nobody in that band, nobody past twenty four hours, nobody placed out of area. A stated absence, such as too few for a median or not drawn in this prototype, means no figure was produced at all. The two are never the same thing, and neither is ever left blank.

Every zero on this page is a real, measured zero unless the words beside it say the figure could not be taken. None of them means not tracked.
