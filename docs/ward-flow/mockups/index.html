Open decisions and things that do not line up

None of these block a merge. They are the questions the build sessions could not settle.

The community team statistics artifact is older than the file.

Its delivery said the artifact and the committed file were byte for byte identical at 294,587 bytes. The file at that commit is 298,900 bytes, and the commit before it is 298,004. Neither matches. The artifact was published before the review fix that made the definition lists valid and named the print control, so the branch is the newer copy and the artifact should not be treated as the same page.

Two deliveries cite a rebuild brief that is not in the repository.

Both name third-edition-kit/REBUILD-BRIEF.md as the contract they were built to. That path does not exist on main or on any of the delivery branches. Only MERGE-BRIEF.md is there. Either the brief was never committed or it was renamed, and the pages that cite it have no readable contract.

Does Delays get its own rail item?

The build session recommends yes, in Operations after Movement. Until that is settled the page borrows Movement's rail position, which is why its rail count reads 16 rather than Command's 14. The sixteen are its own stuck people, not Command's movements.

Five decisions sit on the Bed board.

Bed 09's control reads "They have left" rather than "Confirm the discharge", bed 10 is a held bed with no reason recorded, the figure band of section 6.16 has no rules in the shared stylesheet so the page writes its own, two shell sentences still assume Command's referral queue, and Escape clears a chosen bed only after the shell's own steps. All five are listed in the page's own foot.

Three decisions sit on the emergency department statistics.

Whether Export should be the page's primary action, the size ladder for the bar title, and whether the waiting figure should read 13 or 14.

Ownership is a word on Delays, not a colour.

The source page carried whose-move as five hues down each row's left edge. The third edition gives colour four jobs and ownership is not one of them, so the owner is named instead and the left edge carries severity. Worth a look before it becomes the pattern.

What was checked here, and what was taken on trust.

  • Checked: every branch, commit, path, byte count and sha256 above was read from the repository with git cat-file and sha256sum on 9 September 2026. Pull request numbers, titles and open state were read from GitHub the same day.
  • Checked: the eleven screen names, their groups and their purposes were read out of the rail definition in command-third-edition.html, not from any summary of it.
  • Taken on trust: every harness result. No page listed here was re-run through third-edition-kit/check.mjs for this index. The 52 PASS claims are each build session's own report.
  • Taken on trust: the size and hash of Delays, and everything about Movement. Neither branch is on the remote, so neither file could be read.
  • Not checked: whether the artifacts still resolve, and whether any artifact other than the community team one matches its committed file.
