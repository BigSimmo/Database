## How to read the STATUS column, because it is the only part that matters

- **TESTED** — a check RAN that WOULD HAVE FAILED if the claim were false. Not "a test passed".
  Wherever a mutation established it, the mutation and its restore are named.
- **OBSERVED** — I looked at the thing itself (a rendered screen, a file's bytes, a command's output).
- **REASONED** — I read code or history and concluded. No check ran. Most of this register is REASONED
  and that is fine, as long as nobody later reads it as TESTED.
- **REFUTED** — I asserted it, someone measured, and I was wrong. These are kept, not deleted.

⚠️ **Anything not marked TESTED could be wrong.** Three rows below are corrections to myself and one
is a correction of a claim I had already persuaded another chat to write into a shared document.

## The one thing I would tell the next person

⚠️ **Every one of my worst rows is a case where the check I ran could not have failed.** A grep for a
name that is assembled at runtime. A count of mentions instead of a measurement. Three positive
controls that all exercised the same easy shape. A regex whose lost escape made it match more, not
less. **None of them were careless — each looked exactly like evidence at the time.** The only
defence that has actually worked here is asking, before believing a green result: _what would this
check look like if the thing I am claiming were false?_ If the answer is "the same", it is not
evidence yet.
