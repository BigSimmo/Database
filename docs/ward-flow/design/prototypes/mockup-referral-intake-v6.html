What sending does

Sending records the request and puts it in front of a coordinator.

It places nobody: a coordinator decides every placement, one at a time.

Maya’s name is not written into the structured referral — the receiving team opens her record through the pointer, where access is theirs to have or not. Whatever you typed in the history is sent as written, including a name if you put one there.

This form is not a medical device. It records a request and a written account of it. It does not assess, score, rank or diagnose, and no field on it holds a figure from the Mental Health Act.

7 · Rules the build must not break

Each of these is here because breaking it has already caused a defect somewhere on this system.

  1. Tap targets stay at 48 px. Not 44. Reducing them to satisfy a generic accessibility rule reintroduces a known test flake.
  2. The reason sits beside the Send button, never above it. Reasons appear and disappear as you answer; a reason above the button moves the button out from under a thumb.
  3. A figure beside a checkbox stays beside that checkbox, and a destination’s question stays on that destination’s card. The screen-reader announcement points at it; moving it into a rail silently unhooks the description.
  4. Absence is written in words. “Not recorded”, “Not written yet”, never a blank, never a dash, never a zero. A blank reads as a value.
  5. Nothing on this screen ranks, scores or suggests a destination, and nothing scores the history. Ordering is the network’s own fixed order, and the copy says so.
  6. The name never enters the STRUCTURED referral — and the history is not structured. Say which half is enforced, every time it is said.
  7. Every list of options is generated from the one source of truth, never retyped into a sentence. Retyped enumerations produced stale copy three times — including the sentence on this very screen that counted the permitted facts and was still saying “five” after a sixth was added.
  8. The rail reports the history; it never previews it. A truncated preview implies something read it.
  9. Free text is never given a green state. Green means checked on this screen, and nothing checks this.
  10. Nothing truncates the history silently. Over the limit is a blocking, visible, counted state.
  11. When more than one destination is chosen, the screen says the history goes to all of them, before the send, not after.
  12. Removing a statement from the screen relocates it; it does not delete it. v6 took the banner off the top of the page at the owner’s request. The prototype tag, the free-text warning and the not-a-medical-device sentence all still exist, in three places a reader actually reaches. Check this rule before removing any other sentence for reasons of appearance.

8 · Open questions the build must settle

Written down here rather than promised on screen. A screen that guarantees behaviour nobody has built is the defect this project finds most often.

What happens to a half-written history if the page reloads? Today it is lost — a reload wipes everything. Tolerable for three checkboxes, not tolerable for four paragraphs of a risk note. Either the draft is held and the screen says so, or the screen warns before leaving. It must not silently do neither.
Can the history be edited after sending, and does the receiving team see that it changed? Unanswered. If it can be edited, the version the receiving team read must remain recoverable, or two people will be acting on different accounts of the same patient.
Who can read the history after it is sent, and for how long? The structured fields carry no identifying content, so access has been a low-stakes question. The history changes that: it is the first thing on this form that would matter if the wrong person read it. Needs an answer before a real patient, not before it is built.
Are the three sections the right three, and are the limits right? Chosen as the smallest set that shapes the writing without becoming a clinical form: why now, background, risk and safety. 1500 / 2000 / 1000 characters are placeholders. Nobody has measured a real referral against them. Both are clinical judgements and belong to the owner.

Mockup only — nothing is wired, and every person, bed count, wait, median and line of history above is invented. This form is not a medical device. The screen is drawn mid-flight on purpose, so answered, outstanding, not-applicable, in-catchment, out-of-catchment, refused, written and unwritten states are all visible at once. The patient box’s fields are the ones a person record genuinely holds; where a record does not hold one, it says so in words rather than leaving a gap. Dark mode is defined across the full triple, print and forced-colours rules are defined, and no state on this screen is carried by colour alone.
