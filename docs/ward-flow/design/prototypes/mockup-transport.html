How to book transport

The short path from a patient needing to move to a job appearing on this page.

  1. Open the patient's record and choose "Request transport" from the actions menu.
  2. Choose the origin and destination from the ward, emergency department or home list — never free text.
  3. Mark "Escort required" if the patient is on an involuntary order or is otherwise a risk during transport.
  4. Submit. The request goes to the provider rostered for that route and appears here as "Requested".

Every figure here is invented

  • All totals: 9 jobs waiting or moving, 3 waiting on a provider, the 1d 6h longest wait, 4 escort-required jobs still waiting, 13 finished today, and the 7-day volume bars.
  • Every job: TR-005, TR-009, TR-012, TR-014, TR-018, TR-021, TR-027, TR-032, TR-041 and TR-047 — movement IDs, stages, wait times, timestamps, escort flags and cancellation reasons are all invented.
  • Every provider name — Metro Patient Transport, Regional Ambulance, Community Transport Service, Secure Transfer Service — is a generic, deliberately synthetic name, not a real Western Australian operator.
  • "Updated 2 minutes ago" and the "Live" marker. Nothing here refreshes.

What is real

  • Every hospital, ward and emergency department named — Royal Perth Hospital (RPH Adult Secure, RPH Older Adult), Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital (SCGH Adult Open, SCGH Older Adult), Fiona Stanley Hospital (FSH Adult Secure, FSH Older Adult), Armadale Health Service (ARM Adult Open), St John of God Midland Public Hospital (SJGM Adult Open) and Rockingham General Hospital — comes from this repository's ward model. No ward or hospital here is invented.
  • The missing phone number is real. The model does not store contact details for a transport provider, and the panel says so rather than inventing a number somebody might ring.
  • The escort-required flag is a real field on every transport job in the model. The screen this mockup replaces never showed it. It is shown on every row where it is set.

What changed and why

  • Sorted worst-wait-first, not by movement ID, because the question this page answers is "who has been waiting too long", not "show me everything in order".
  • The provider is bold on every row because it is who you contact when a job is stuck. The two panels split active jobs from finished ones so a long list of history never buries what still needs a decision.
  • The two counts that used to be buried mid-sentence in a banner are now the "Jobs waiting for pickup or moving now" and "Finished today" tiles, each a real figure rather than a clause in a sentence.
  • This page does not attempt to prove any provider is slow. That comparison belongs on Statistics; this page only ever shows what is happening right now.
