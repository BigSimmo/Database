6 · Rules the build must not break

Each of these is here because breaking it has already caused a defect somewhere on this system.

  1. Tap targets stay at 48 px. Not 44. Reducing them to satisfy a generic accessibility rule reintroduces a known test flake.
  2. The reason sits beside the Send button, never above it. Reasons appear and disappear as you answer; a reason above the button moves the button out from under a thumb.
  3. A figure beside a checkbox stays beside that checkbox. The screen reader announcement points at it; moving it into a rail silently unhooks the description.
  4. Absence is written in words. “Not recorded”, never a blank, never a dash, never a zero. A blank reads as a value.
  5. Nothing on this screen ranks, scores or suggests a destination. Ordering is the network’s own fixed order, and the copy says so.
  6. The person’s name never enters the referral. It may be shown; it may not be stored.
  7. Every list of options is generated from the one source of truth, never retyped into a sentence. Retyped enumerations are how this system produced stale copy three times.

Mockup only — nothing is wired, and every person, bed count, wait and median above is invented. The screen is drawn mid-flight on purpose, so answered, outstanding, not-applicable, in-catchment, out-of-catchment and refused states are all visible at once. The anchor’s fields are the ones a person record genuinely holds; where a record does not hold one, the anchor says so in words rather than leaving a gap. Dark mode is defined for every token and follows the reader’s system setting.
