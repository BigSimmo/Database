Decisions this redesign takes

  • The bed map replaces the link. “See every bed on this ward” was one underlined sentence; it is now the largest thing on the page, and the board link is a button inside it.
  • No control stretches. Every button is the width of its own words. The full-bleed “Confirm capacity” bar is now a three-character field with a small button beside it.
  • Three columns, one job each — what the ward has, what the ward is asked to say, and what is waiting on a person here. The old page was a single stack roughly nine screens tall.
  • Referrals move above the fold. They were the last thing on the page; a ward's own beds and the people waiting for them now sit side by side.
  • Absences are drawn. Out-of-service and on-leave keep their place in the key, and every empty panel says what its emptiness means.
