Every name here is invented

The eight people, their stories, plans and reviews are synthetic. The UMRNs come from the prototype’s own seed data (ward-patients-seed.ts) and the form codes are the real Western Australian ones the model offers: 1A · 3B · 3D · 4A · 4C.

A blank is a blank, not a zero

“Not seen today” means no review was recorded. It never means the person was not seen. This board is a record of what was written down and must never be read as a record of care.

Why durations and not dates

The prototype’s day zero reads the real clock, so a printed date would drift while a duration does not. Nothing on this page will silently go stale.

What is real

Every identifier, wait, tier, destination and state was read off the running prototype at /mockups/ward-flow/ed/rph-ed on 2026-09-04. Department and ward names come from ward-sites.ts.

What is invented

The layout, the bar lengths as a proportion of the 24-hour target, the named actions and everything in the last-24-hours list. The underlying figures are the screen’s own.

Two different clocks

Coming in is measured from the referral; going out from when the move became owed. They are drawn on separate axes and never ranked against one another, because they are not the same quantity.

Why this is empty

Nothing is missing from the record. Nothing has been drawn. Royal Perth’s board was read off the running prototype. The other seven would have to be invented, and a prototype that invents seven departments’ waits, decisions and outstanding times is one somebody will eventually read as a measurement.

The switch itself is the thing to judge here, not the contents: whether one press is the right way to move between departments, and whether the code alone is enough to know where you have landed once the page has changed underneath you.
