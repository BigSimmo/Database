Nothing can be listed here, and that is a gap in the record rather than a quiet week. A referral DOES name the team it is addressed to — teamName, required on every community destination, and that is how everyone else on this page is here. What it does not carry is which team RAISED it: the source side records only that a referral came from a community service. So a referral Midland sent out cannot be traced back to Midland, and every row this module could show would be a guess.

⚠ An earlier version of this panel blamed an unbuilt owner ruling, and that was wrong. The 2026-08-31 ruling — that a patient is a team’s by an explicit team on the referral, not by home area — IS built; teamName exists because of it. The missing half is the source side of the same record, and nobody has ruled on that one.

Where these teams come from

Every team listed comes from one extracted source document, no team has agreed to be represented, and nothing here has been checked against a real service. The names are real; no contact detail is held, and none is invented.

What is invented

Every patient, bed and date, and everything in the last-24-hours list. The suburb count and every team name are real.

Not a caseload

An empty list means nobody has been recorded as followed up, not that nobody is being followed up. This page must never be read as a record of who a team is looking after.
