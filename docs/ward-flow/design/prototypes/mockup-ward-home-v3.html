Decisions this edition takes, and one correction

  • The ward changed, and this is the correction. The previous mockup drew SCGH Adult Open as 6 locked, 18 open, copied from the running screen. On this branch that ward has no locked beds at all: commit f08548f1b reverted invented locked beds in two open wards because they made a detained patient appear placeable somewhere he could not lawfully be held. Rather than re-draw the reverted data, this edition moves to a ward that is genuinely mixed in the fixture.
  • The bed map is the page. It is grouped into the locked bay and the open beds, so the fact that decides a detained patient’s placement is a heading rather than a word repeated on eighteen tiles.
  • A real accent colour. The first edition had only alarm colours, so nothing could be calm and important at once. This is PsychSift’s own primary, which is what makes the two products look like one thing.
  • Crisp white surfaces. No panel is tinted. State is carried by an edge, a word and a colour — never by washing a large surface in cream.
  • The ward switcher replaces a plain dropdown. A flat alphabetical list of 23 names could not show a ward’s designation or how many beds it has ready, and could not be searched. This one groups by hospital, carries the ready count, and marks the two unauthorised wards.
  • Every colour pairing here was computed, not judged. The quiet text colour was moved to #5e6c84 because the obvious value measured 4.41:1 on the page ground, below the 4.5:1 floor.
