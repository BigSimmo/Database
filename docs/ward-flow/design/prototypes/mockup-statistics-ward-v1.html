What this page is

  • A single ward’s own statistics, reached from that ward’s home screen. Nothing on it aggregates across wards, and nothing on it writes anything back — it is read-only.
  • SCGH Adult Open’s identity — Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital, adult, 24 beds, all open, no locked bay — is taken from the ward switcher on the approved mockup-ward-home-v4.html prototype, so the two do not contradict each other if the owner opens both. Nothing else on this page is drawn from that or any other fixture: every statistic below is invented for this prototype alone.

What is invented

  • Every figure on this page — the bed split, the 30 daily occupancy counts, all 21 lengths of stay and their average and median, every admission and discharge count and the 14 daily counts behind the two sparklines, the discharge-planning breakdown, the four reasons a patient is ready to leave but blocked, all four referral counts, and the three long-stay references and their day counts.
  • This ward’s current time, “confirmed 21:48,” and the reporting window, “last 30 days ending today.”

How the numbers agree with each other

  • 24 beds = 21 occupied + 2 available + 1 out of service.
  • The 21 patients currently on the ward = 6 under 2 weeks + 7 at 2 weeks–1 month + 5 at 1–3 months + 3 over 3 months — and those 3 are the same 3 long stays listed under “Long stays.”
  • 22 referrals received this month = 15 accepted + 4 declined + 3 still open. The 15 accepted are the same 15 counted in this month’s 15 admissions.
  • 13 discharges this month = 8 that met their recorded date + 4 whose date was moved + 1 with no date recorded.
  • 9 patients with a discharge date recorded + 12 without = the full 21 currently on the ward.
  • The occupancy chart’s last point, 21, matches “Occupied” above. Its value 7 days earlier, 20, is 1 fewer than today — the same net gain as 4 admissions against 3 discharges this week. Its value 30 days earlier, 19, is 2 fewer than today — the same net gain as 15 admissions against 13 discharges this month.
  • Acceptance rate 68% is 15 ÷ 22, rounded to the nearest whole per cent.

What this page does not know

This ward does not yet record where a discharge went — home, another inpatient service, or a community bed — so that split is stated in the discharge-planning panel in words, with no invented number attached to it. That is different from a zero: a zero here means the count was taken and came back none; this means the count was never possible to take.

Not a caseload, and not a waiting list

  • “Ready to leave but blocked” lists the reasons recorded today. It is never a target for how many should be freed, and a lower number next week is not, by itself, evidence that anything changed for the better.
  • A zero anywhere on this page is a real, measured zero unless a caption beside it says the figure could not be taken. It is never used to mean “not tracked.”
