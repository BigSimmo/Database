What is real

  • RPH Adult Secure, Royal Perth Hospital, Locked, Adult, 20 beds — from this repository's ward model (ward-sites.ts).
  • 2 beds empty, 1 allocatable, 1 free — the same source. The free figure on the primary button is the smaller of empty and allocatable, exactly as the model defines it.

Why this page works this way

  • Confirming is one tap; changing is the extra step. The two bed-count questions share a single "nothing has changed" button, because they are normally reported together every shift. Only opening "Change the number" costs more than a glance — try the button above.
  • The two kinds of question decay differently. Bed counts turn stale in hours because they change every shift. A ward's intake constraints change far less often, so a nurse is never punished for not re-answering something that has not moved — but here it has never been answered at all, which is a different problem from staleness and is worded differently: "Never answered" rather than "Stale".
  • "Open bed list" never waits for the form. It sits above every question, shows the real free-bed count, and works whether every question is answered or not. What changes is the sentence beside it, which states plainly how old that count is and who is relying on it.
  • The gap between empty and allocatable is shown, not collapsed. A ward with 2 beds physically empty but only 1 it can actually give away is a different fact from "1 free bed", and the coordinator on the other end needs both numbers, not just the smaller one.
