The locked ward mockups

This is the style block of the three ward mockups that carry the second edition — mockup-ward-home-v4.html (the ward home the owner locked in on 2026-09-05, and the one the build follows), mockup-ward-board-v3.html, and mockup-ward-home-v3.html, which is kept as the record of what was approved earlier that night and is no longer the spec. To change it, edit this file and re-cut the mockups; do not hand-edit a copy.

The three mockups were re-cut from this file rather than patched.
