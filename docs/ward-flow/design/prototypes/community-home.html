  • The missing phone number is real. The model does not store one, and the panel says so rather than inventing a number somebody might ring.

How the two scopes work

  • All teams is the coordinator's landing. Both columns are sorted worst-wait-first rather than by team, because a coordinator's question is "what is going wrong", not "show me a list". The team is named on every row.
  • The teams table at the foot is the filter — and it earns its place by carrying the counts, so you can see which team to open before you open it. An amber edge marks any team with somebody waiting over three days. "Open hub" switches the whole screen to that team.
  • The team select at the top does the same thing for when you already know where you are going.
  • Three teams show "none" rather than 0. A nought reads as a measurement; "none" reads as a state, and the attention panel flags idle teams as worth checking rather than as good news.
