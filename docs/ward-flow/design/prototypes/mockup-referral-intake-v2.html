Synthetic prototype

This form is not a medical device. It records only the five permitted facts about the person referred, the request itself, and — when the referral was raised from a person’s record — a pointer to that record: an id and nothing else. Never a name, never free text, and never a figure from the Mental Health Act.

What sending does

Sending records the request and queues it for a coordinator.

It places nobody: a coordinator decides every placement, one at a time. Nothing on this page chooses a destination for you.

Mockup only — nothing here is wired. Shown mid-flight so the answered, outstanding, in-catchment, out-of-catchment and refused-combination states are all visible at once; a finished-looking mockup hides exactly the states a referrer actually meets. Every bed figure, wait and median is invented. Colours, spacing, radii and the elevation ladder are taken from mockupwardhomev3 so the two screens read as one product.
