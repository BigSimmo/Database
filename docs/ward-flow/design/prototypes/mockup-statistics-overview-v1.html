The totals row is not a column sum for the longest and median wait. Longest is the single longest wait anywhere in the network (31h, Royal Perth Hospital ED); median is the middle wait time across all 34 people waiting, not an average of the eight departments’ own medians. Waiting, over 8h and over 24h are true sums — and over 24h is a subset of over 8h, not an addition to it. A 0 in this table is a measured zero — Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital ED, Joondalup Health Campus ED, Armadale Hospital ED, St John of God Midland ED, Rockingham General Hospital ED and Peel Health Campus ED genuinely have nobody who has waited over 24 hours right now, not a missing figure.

What is real, taken from the repository

  • 23 wards across 15 hospitals, and their exact bed counts. Every ward name, hospital name and bed count on this page — including the eight named in “Where the pressure is” — is read from ward-sites.ts. 303 total beds is the arithmetic sum of those 23 real counts, not an invented figure.
  • 8 emergency departments, also from ward-sites.ts: Royal Perth, Sir Charles Gairdner, Fiona Stanley, Armadale, St John of God Midland, Rockingham General, Joondalup Health Campus and Peel Health Campus. Joondalup and Peel run an emergency department but hold no mental health beds of their own, which is why neither appears in the ward tables — the same fact the ward home screen’s ward switcher states.
  • The 17 community team names and their suburb counts — Midland at 68 down to Wheat Belt at 10 — are the same list already used on the approved community hub screen, which states they are the teams ward-catchment.ts gives ten or more suburbs. That list was reused here rather than re-derived independently from the catchment table for this page.
  • The shared visual language — every colour, spacing value, panel, table, chip and figure-tile rule above the marker in this file — is the canonical block, copied unedited from design-language.html.

What is invented

  • Every bed's state: occupied, ready, held and out of service, on every one of the 23 wards, including the eight shown. These were chosen so the 23 wards' invented splits sum exactly to this page's own invented system totals — 19 ready, 8 held, 9 out of service, 267 occupied, out of the 303 real beds — but the arrangement across individual wards is invented, not measured. Only the 303-bed ceiling is real.
  • Every wait, admission, discharge, referral and caseload figure on the page: the 14-day flow chart and its 188 admissions and 157 discharges; today's 14 admissions and 11 discharges; all 34 people waiting in an emergency department and their wait times, including the 31-hour longest wait and the 9-hour median; the 22 referrals raised today and their 9 accepted, 4 declined and 9 still open; and every community team's caseload, new-referral and discharge figure, including the 1,064, 66 and 53 totals across all 17 teams.
  • The “referred, awaiting an answer” counts in “Where the pressure is” are a separate invented figure from the emergency-department waits opposite — the two counts are stated as different populations in the panel's own text precisely so they are not added together by a reader who assumes they are the same thing.
  • The “confirmed 14:20 by 15 of 15 hospitals” liveness marker at the top of the page, and every kpi-delta ("+6 on yesterday", "+3 on yesterday").

What this screen deliberately does not do

  • It has no switcher, and no per-ward, per-department or per-team action. This is the top of the hierarchy the ward, ED and community switchers route out of; every figure here links onward to the service it belongs to rather than acting on it directly.
  • It does not claim the 23-ward invented split is a measurement. The heading figures and the totals rows are arithmetic on invented per-ward numbers, not a live feed — stated once here rather than repeated on every figure.
  • A zero anywhere on this page is a real, measured zero unless a caption beside it says the figure could not be taken. It is never used to mean “not tracked.”
