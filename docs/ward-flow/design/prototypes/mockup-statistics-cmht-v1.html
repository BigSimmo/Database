What is invented

  • This team’s name, the health service it sits under, and the seven suburbs listed — Armadale is not one of the seventeen community team names already drawn on mockup-community-hub-v3.html (Midland, Bunbury, Joondalup and the rest), chosen deliberately so this page invents its own identity rather than reusing, and possibly contradicting, a team whose own suburb count and figures already appear elsewhere.
  • Every operational figure on this page — the 142 open cases and their four duration bands, this month’s new and closed cases, all referral counts and decline reasons, the referral-source table, the hospital-discharge figures, the time-to-first-contact average, median and four-band distribution, every contact count and the 7 daily figures behind the sparkline, and the four hospital-bed references and their day counts.
  • This team’s current time, “confirmed 14:20,” and the reporting window, “this month so far.”

How the numbers agree with each other

  • 142 people currently open to the team = 38 open under 3 months + 51 at 3 months–1 year + 39 at 1–3 years + 14 over 3 years.
  • 19 new cases opened this month minus 16 cases closed this month = a net change of +3.
  • 28 referrals received this month = 19 accepted + 5 declined + 4 still open. The 19 accepted are the same 19 counted in this month’s 19 new cases opened.
  • The 5 declined referrals = 2 outside the catchment area + 1 assessed as needing inpatient care + 1 client declined or uncontactable + 1 already open to another team + 0 withdrawn by the referrer.
  • The 28 referrals received, by source, = 9 ward discharge + 7 emergency department + 8 GP + 3 self or family + 1 another community team.
  • 9 people discharged from hospital into this team’s care this month is the same 9 ward-discharge referrals above, and every one of the 9 was accepted.
  • The 19 new cases opened this month, by days to first contact, = 6 within 2 days + 9 at 3–7 days + 3 at 8–14 days + 1 over 14 days.
  • 316 contacts this month = 189 face-to-face + 98 telephone + 29 did not attend.
  • The sparkline’s 7 daily contact counts — 12, 9, 14, 10, 8, 13 and 11 today — sum to the 77 stated in its caption, an exact average of 11 a day.
  • Acceptance rate 68% is 19 ÷ 28, rounded to the nearest whole per cent.

What this page does not know

This team does not yet record which clinician discipline made a given contact or a first contact — whether a psychiatrist, a mental health nurse, a social worker or another allied health clinician saw someone — so neither contacts nor first-contact timing can be broken down that way on this page. That is different from a zero: a zero here means the count was taken and came back none; this means the count was never possible to take.

Not a duty roster, and not a performance target

  • The time-to-first-contact figures describe what happened, not what should happen. No target wait is drawn on this page, and a shorter median next month is not, by itself, evidence that anything changed for the better.
  • A zero anywhere on this page is a real, measured zero unless a caption beside it says the figure could not be taken. It is never used to mean “not tracked.”
