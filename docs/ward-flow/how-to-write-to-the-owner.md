# How to write to the owner

Settled by the owner on 2026-09-01 after re-explaining it to several chats in turn. **That
re-explaining is the problem this file exists to end** — a new chat should inherit the format here
rather than be told it again.

## The shape

**Detail first, at whatever length the subject genuinely needs.** This is not a brevity rule: a
verification report should take the space it warrants. Then a horizontal rule, the heading
`Summary`, then the labelled lines.

    DID        what you actually did this turn, including work not discussed above. Say plainly
               whether it worked, half-worked or failed, and carry the LIMITATION rather than only
               the outcome. A short list when there were several, most consequential first.

    ISSUE      anything wrong or risky, in plain words that scale with severity — "serious",
               "I was wrong". It also owns DANGER: if he is about to do something irreversible it
               says so, and says exactly what would be lost.

    GAPS       what you did NOT check that he might reasonably assume you did.

    NEED       only decisions you genuinely cannot make yourself.

    RECOMMEND  your view on what is in NEED. Kept separate so he sees the question before your
               answer and is not anchored by it.

    NEXT       what you are about to do. Announce, then act.

## Which lines are mandatory

**`ISSUE` and `GAPS` always print, even as one word.** They are the two that protect him, and the
two you will be most tempted to skip on a turn that went well — **their absence would be
indistinguishable from not having considered them.**

`DID` prints whenever you did anything. `NEED`, `RECOMMEND` and `NEXT` print only when they have
content; their absence is unambiguous.

## The two binding properties

1. **The summary must stand alone.** If he reads only the block, he should know what happened, what
   state things are in, and what is waiting on him. Each line needs enough context to make sense
   cold — not merely to summarise the paragraph above it.
2. **The detail EXPLAINS and the block STATES.** If the detail section is a list of points, the
   summary has been written twice and he reads it twice. Explanation up top, position down below.

## Three rules that came out of getting it wrong

- **`ISSUE` can never read "none" while `NEED` points at something permanent.** A question about an
  irreversible act is a risk, not merely a question.
- **`GAPS` must report something new or say none.** Repeating the same gap verbatim every message
  turns the line into wallpaper and he stops reading it — which costs more than never having had it.
- **If you have asked the same thing twice with no answer, stop asking and choose.** Note the choice
  in `DID`. Anything minor and reversible was never his to decide in the first place.

## What the format does not change

**Anything irreversible, or that reaches outside your own chat, still gets a question before you do
it.** A `NEXT` line is a notification, not permission.
