# Decision: Docling shadow extraction starts at 2 percent of documents

- **Status:** decided 2026-08-19
- **Source:** `docs/worker-deploy-runbook.md`, find "It is the owner-approved default recorded in"

When Docling shadow extraction is switched on, it samples 2 percent of documents by default, the bottom of its authorised 1 to 5 percent window.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
