# Decision: On Call section pages show their groups as a visible bar

- **Status:** decided 2026-09-13
- **Source:** `docs/search-chrome-behaviour.md`, find "On Call's section pages are a recorded exception to that last clause (owner decision,"

On Call's section pages show their few one-word groups as a visible bar even though they scroll with a section spy, an exception to the usual in-page navigation rule. Pages with eight or more sections, or with prose labels, still use the weighted track.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
