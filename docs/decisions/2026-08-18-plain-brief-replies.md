# Decision: Replies to the owner are short and plain, and lead with what to do

- **Status:** decided 2026-08-18
- **Source:** `.claude/cloud-profile/memory/communication-style-plain-and-brief.md`, find "asked (2026-08-18) for replies to be much simpler and shorter"

Agents answer the owner in plain English: what happened or what to do first, then numbered steps and one recommendation, with no unexplained jargon. Full detail is kept for destructive actions, exact commands, and when the owner asks for it.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
