# Decision: A differential body known to be wrong is withheld, not caveated

- **Status:** decided 2026-09-16
- **Source:** `src/lib/differential-curated.ts`, find "Owner ruling, 2026-09-16. A note asking the reader to distrust the body of"

When a differential's generated body is known to be wrong, it is withheld rather than shown under a warning note. Showing nothing degrades conservatively; showing another diagnosis's management does not.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
