# Decision: The Patient Plan never claims the person helped write it when they did not

- **Status:** decided 2026-08-25
- **Source:** `docs/care-plan/sdd-ledger.md`, find "D4. The Patient Plan must not tell a person they helped write a plan they took no part in"

The Care Plan prototype's Patient Plan never tells a person they helped write a plan they took no part in, including in its section headings and lead-ins.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
