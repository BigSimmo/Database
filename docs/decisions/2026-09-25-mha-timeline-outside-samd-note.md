# Decision: The Mental Health Act timeline is outside the medical-device note

- **Status:** decided 2026-09-25
- **Source:** `docs/clinical-hazard-analysis.md`, find "TGA/SaMD reading — owner ruling, 2026-09-25."

The Mental Health Act quoted timeline sits outside the open software-as-a-medical-device note, because it calculates a statutory time limit, a legal output rather than a diagnosis or treatment recommendation. The ruling holds only while its controls stay in place, such as calculating times only for owner-signed entries.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
