# Decision: The safety chips widen their urgent words but skip drug-passage wording

- **Status:** decided 2026-09-25
- **Source:** `docs/clinical-hazard-controls.json`, find "signed off widening only the urgent tokens"

The red-flag chip now also matches "urgent", "urgently" and "seizure", and the escalation chip matches forms of "transfer" (#GHC4XZ). Wording about a drug passing into breast milk, across the placenta or into similar places is excluded, because it describes the drug, not moving the patient.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
