# Decision: "Strong support" needs every claim directly supported

- **Status:** decided 2026-09-25
- **Source:** `docs/outstanding-issues.md`, find "owner decisions 3, 10 and 17, 2026-09-25"

An answer shows "Strong support" only when every assessed claim is directly supported by approved or locally reviewed sources, and an answer with no assessed claims reads "Supported" at most (#WGMB4Z). This changes the label only; the other display limits stay as they were.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
