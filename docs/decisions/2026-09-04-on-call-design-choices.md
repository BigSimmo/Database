# Decision: On Call: where its content lives and what it will not do

- **Status:** decided 2026-09-04
- **Source:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-on-call-mode-design.md`, find "Decisions taken, and what they rule out"

On Call content lives in the database and is written and edited in the app; it keeps its own referrals list, treats each uploaded manual as the source of truth instead of retyping it, and builds no rosters. Its playbook only links to the owner's own uploaded guidelines and never authors clinical steps.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
