# Decision: On Call entries are readable by every visitor, except personal numbers

- **Status:** decided 2026-09-04
- **Source:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-on-call-mode-design.md`, find "Amendment — 2026-09-04, owner decision."

On Call entries are readable by anyone who reaches the site, signed in or not, because the site has no signed-in-colleagues tier to choose instead. Entries flagged as personal numbers are never shared, and editing still needs an account.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
