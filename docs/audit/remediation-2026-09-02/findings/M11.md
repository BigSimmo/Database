### M11 · Ingestion quality review loads full page text for up to 200 documents with no row bound, so results silently truncate at the PostgREST row cap

`src/app/api/ingestion/quality/route.ts:362-399 (document_pages select at 391-394; document_images 395-398; ingestion_job_stages 384-390)` · correctness · **CONFIRMED** · safe_cleanup=false · lane L04 (L04-F2)

- **Defect:** The route selects `document_id,page_number,text,ocr_used,metadata` from `document_pages` for every document in the 200-document window with no `.limit()`/`.range()`, and likewise unbounded `document_images`, `ingestion_jobs` and `ingestion_job_stages`. Hosted Supabase applies a 1,000-row response cap (this repo already names it: docs/audit/primary-checkout-reconciliation-2026-07-24.md:92, and `search-scope.ts:29` paginates at 1000 for the same reason). Rows past the cap are dropped without error, and the heuristics at lines 214-217 and 254-258 (`lowTextPages`, `needsOcrPages`, `tablesWithoutRows`) then count against a partial page set. The full `text` column is also shipped over the wire only to be measured with `.trim().length < 80`.
- **Trigger:** Administrator opens the ingestion quality console (`limit` defaults to 120, max 200) on a corpus where the newest 120–200 documents hold more than 1,000 pages combined — routine for clinical guidelines (repo-awareness snapshot: 582 documents).
- **Impact:** The 'image-only pages need review' and 'table extraction needs review' items are computed from whichever ≤1,000 page/image rows PostgREST returned, so documents later in the window can be reported as clean when they are not; the response also transfers megabytes of page text per request (latency, memory).
- **Proof:** not executed — traced: route.ts:391-394 `supabase.from("document_pages").select("document_id,page_number,text,ocr_used,metadata").in("document_id", documentIds)` with no limit; `grep -n max_rows supabase/config.toml` → no override; tests/ingestion-quality-route.test.ts mocks the chain and cannot observe the cap. Live row counts not verifiable offline.
- **Fix sketch:** Replace the page read with an RPC/aggregate (per-document counts of pages with `length(text) < 80` and `metadata->>'needsOcr'`), or page the child reads in 1,000-row windows and mark the response `partial` when a window fills; never select `text` when only its length is needed.
- **Skeptic (CONFIRMED):** Traced end-to-end with quoted code; the 1,000-row cap is asserted as fact by the repo itself and already caused #075. Only the exact live page count of the newest 120-200 documents is unmeasured, but with 2,851 guideline documents in the corpus exceeding 1,000 pages across 120 documents is near-certain. tests/ingestion-quality-route.test.ts mocks the query chain and cannot observe truncation. Impact is confined to the authenticated owner's quality console (owner-scoped read), so it caps at Medium; kept Medium rather than Low because the console silently reports later documents as clean. Fix (aggregate RPC or paged reads) does not touch RAG ranking, ledgers, migrations-safety or diff-integrity floors; a new RPC would be a migration and must follow the merge-window rule.
