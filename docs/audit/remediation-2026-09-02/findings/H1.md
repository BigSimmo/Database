> **Correction (Stage 5, red team):** The cited consumer lines do not call formulationShortLabel (module-private, medication-badges.ts:84); they call medicationIdentityBadges/medicationAccessBadges, which reach it at :247 and :319. Substance unchanged: the committed snapshot reproduces varenicline/meloxicam/methadone → '5 mg tablet', nicotine-lozenge → '5 mg'.

- **Defect:** The regex captures only the integer run immediately before "mg", so a decimal strength is read from its fractional digits, and a concentration such as "5 mg/mL" is read as a 5 mg unit strength. The label is then rendered as an identity badge ("5 mg tablet") in the record hero, the prescribing result rows and cross-mode links.
- **Trigger:** Any snapshot record whose Route/formulation quick row or Oral routes form row states a decimal or per-mL strength before the first integer-mg strength. Present in the committed data today: varenicline "Tablets (0.5 mg, 1 mg)" → "5 mg tablet"; meloxicam "Tablets (7.5 mg, 15 mg)" → "5 mg tablet"; nicotine-lozenge "Lozenges (1.5 mg, 2 mg, 4 mg)" → "5 mg"; buprenorphine-naloxone "films (2/0.5 mg and 8/2 mg)" → "5 mg"; methadone "Oral liquid syrup (5 mg/mL). Tablets (10 mg)" → "5 mg tablet"; cetirizine/loratadine "Oral liquid (1 mg/mL)" cases similar.
- **Impact:** A clinician opening varenicline or meloxicam sees a strength badge for a tablet that does not exist (varenicline is 0.5 mg / 1 mg; meloxicam 7.5 mg / 15 mg) — a tenfold error on the record's most prominent line. The full text is still in the sections below, but the badge is the glanceable value the hero is designed around, and the same label appears on prescribing result rows.
- **Proof:** not executed in-app — traced, then reproduced offline: copied the function body verbatim into `node -e` over data/medications-snapshot.json; 181 quick/oral-routes values produce a label that is not the first strength in the text, 8 of them by dropping a decimal (list above) and ~40 by reading an mg/mL concentration as a tablet strength. tests/medication-badges.test.ts only asserts the integer case ("333 mg EC tablet", line 28) and passed (77/77 in the focused run). The repo already knows this class of bug: src/lib/medications.ts:224-226 documents `firstClinicalSentence` as fixed for "1.5 mg", but formulationShortLabel was not.
- **Fix sketch:** Match `(\d+(?:\.\d+)?)\s*(mg|mcg|µg|g)(?!\s*/)` (exclude per-mL/per-5mL concentrations, keep the unit that was matched), or emit no badge when the first strength token is a concentration or a combination ("2/0.5 mg"). Add unit cases for 0.5 mg, 7.5 mg, 5 mg/mL and 2/0.5 mg.
- **Caution:** Do not widen to a first-number fallback — the generator comment at scripts/build-medication-interaction-index.ts:119-124 explains why bare first-token heuristics misfire on this corpus.
- **Skeptic (CONFIRMED):** Line reads exactly as claimed and the trigger is present in committed data; the trailing-parenthetical strip at :87 does not help because the strength parenthetical is followed by more text (varenicline) or a brand parenthetical (meloxicam). A tenfold-wrong strength on the hero identity badge is a number a clinician can act on, so High stands.
