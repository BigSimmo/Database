### M9 · Safety-note text cleaner deletes ordinary hyphenated phrases after 'protocol/policy/procedure', changing clinical meaning

`src/lib/clinical-safety.ts:67` · correctness · **CONFIRMED** · safe_cleanup=false · lane L02 (L02-F1)

- **Defect:** conciseSourceText() strips document-code noise with `/\b(?:procedure|policy|protocol)\s+[A-Z]{2,}(?:-[A-Z0-9]+)+(?:\/\d+)?\b[\s.:-]*/gi`. The `i` flag makes `[A-Z]{2,}(?:-[A-Z0-9]+)+` match any lowercase hyphenated word, so the regex — intended for codes like `Procedure PAE-PRO-0338/16` (the only shape tests/clinical-safety.test.ts:124 pins) — also removes real words: 'protocol re-challenge', 'policy co-prescribing', 'procedure post-operative'.
- **Trigger:** Any grounded answer whose quote card or source chunk contains a hyphenated word immediately after 'protocol', 'policy' or 'procedure' (common in guideline prose: re-challenge, co-prescribing, post-operative, pre-dose, non-urgent). The text then feeds the Safety findings chips (extractSafetyFindings) on the answer page.
