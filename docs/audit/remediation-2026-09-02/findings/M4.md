> **Correction (Stage 5, red team):** Merge into one finding. docs/codebase-index.md:794 is :793 on this branch after commit 2's enumeration edit.

- **Defect:** The Prescribing patient profile (age, eGFR/CrCl/creatinine, hepatic grade, QTc, pregnancy, breastfeeding, allergy classes, up to 40 current medications) is kept in sessionStorage under `clinical-kb-patient-profile`. Nothing removes that key except closing the tab: the sign-out, markSessionExpired and user-id-change handlers clear recent queries, the answer thread and the signed-URL cache but not the profile, and the settings dialog's privacy "Clear" only calls clearRecentQueries (settings-dialog.tsx:609,626).
- **Trigger:** Shared workstation: clinician A enters a patient's physiology on /medications, signs out (or the session expires); clinician B signs in in the same tab.
- **Impact:** Clinician B's medication pages immediately evaluate renal/hepatic/QTc/pregnancy/interaction alerts against patient A's physiology and medication list, with the Patient details pill pre-filled — a wrong-patient decision-support context that looks intentional. The data is de-identified physiology, but it is patient-specific clinical context the app's own sign-out boundary comment (recent-query-storage.ts:78-80) says must not be inherited by the next person.
- **Proof:** grep -rn 'clinical-kb-patient-profile|PATIENT_PROFILE_STORAGE_KEY|removeItem(' src → the key is written at patient-profile-storage.ts:187 and never removed anywhere; client.tsx:390-402 enumerates exactly clearPersistedAnswerThread(), clearRecentQueries(), clearSignedUrlCache(). docs/codebase-index.md:794 documents it as 'cleared on tab close' only. Not executed — traced.
- **Fix sketch:** Add `clearPatientProfile()` (removeItem + dispatch PATIENT_PROFILE_CHANGE_EVENT) in patient-profile-storage.ts and call it beside clearRecentQueries() in the sign-out, markSessionExpired and user-id-change paths of client.tsx; also include it in the settings privacy Clear. Optionally clear `clinical-kb-search-pins-v1` (search-pins.ts:31, localStorage, unscoped) at the same boundary — low sensitivity (pin names/destinations only).
- **Skeptic (CONFIRMED):** Every cited line confirms: the profile (age, renal/hepatic, QTc, pregnancy, allergies, medications) persists in sessionStorage across sign-out, expiry and account switch within the same tab, while the repo's own sign-out boundary clears every other clinical-context store.
