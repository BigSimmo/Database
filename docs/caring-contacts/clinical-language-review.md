## 5. Approval status and residual risk

The content is suitable for a synthetic design handoff only. Final sender identity, programme phone,
hours, emergency wording, crisis contact, message variants and translated content require versioned
local clinical-programme and lived-experience/content approval. Privacy/security, records,
integration and service governance may block production. This review does not authorise patient
data, SMS delivery, clinical use or a pilot.
