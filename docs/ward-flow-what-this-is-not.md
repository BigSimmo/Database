# Ward Flow — what this is NOT

⛔ **READ THIS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE IN THIS PROTOTYPE.**

**Ward Flow is a design prototype. It is not software in use anywhere, by anyone, for any purpose.**

## ⛔ It is not clinical software

- ⛔ **It is NOT validated clinical decision support**, and must never be used to make, inform, or
  record a decision about a real person.
- ⛔ **It is NOT in service.** No health service uses it. It has never been deployed, piloted, or
  trialled.
- ⛔ **It has had no clinical governance review, no safety review, no privacy review, and no
  regulatory assessment of any kind.**
- ⛔ **It reaches no real system.** No database, no patient administration system, no bed registry,
  no messaging, no network.

## ⛔ Every patient in it is invented

**The eight patient records — names, dates of birth, UMRNs, legal status, Mental Health Act form
codes — are synthetic throughout. None corresponds to any real person, living or dead.**

⚠️ **The surnames are DELIBERATELY name-shaped rather than obvious placeholders**, because the
prototype must demonstrate related-name search and that requires genuine near-misses to find —
`Halloway` beside `Hallowin`, `Marrowby` beside `Marrowbee`. **They were invented so that no real
person matches.** Dates of birth are fixed rather than computed. UMRNs are sequential fakes.

> ⛔ **THIS IS THE PART MOST EASILY MISREAD FROM OUTSIDE. A screen of realistic Australian names,
> with dates of birth and hospital numbers and detention status, LOOKS EXACTLY LIKE A PATIENT
> LIST.** ✅ **It is not one, and it never was.**

## ⚠️ Why it looks more finished than it is

**The prototype is deliberately built to look like working software, because its purpose is to show
a clinician what a coordination hub could feel like to use.** ⚠️ **That realism is the point, and it
is also the risk: a reader arriving without context has nothing on the page telling them it is a
sketch.**

**It is incomplete in ways that are not visible from the screens:** whole surfaces are unbuilt,
several values are placeholders awaiting real-world data, and the underlying data is a small fixed
fixture rather than anything live.

## ✅ No Mental Health Act figure is invented anywhere

**A standing constraint of this work, enforced throughout: no figure, timeframe or threshold from
the Mental Health Act is invented anywhere in this prototype.** **Form codes appear as NAMES only —
`No form`, `1A`, `3B`, `3D`, `4A`, `4C` — with no durations, no limits and no statutory periods
attached to them.**

⚠️ **Where a real system would show a statutory clock, this prototype shows the passage of time
against a placeholder the owner has not yet supplied.**

## ✅ What it IS

**A synthetic, offline design prototype of a psychiatric bed-flow coordination hub, built to explore
what such a system might need to show and to whom** — **and, as much as anything, to find out what
questions have to be answered before anybody builds a real one.**
